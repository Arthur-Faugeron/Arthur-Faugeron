### About Me

I'm a quant student working at the intersection of mathematics, markets, and code. My interests span from the theoretical foundations of stochastic processes to the practical mechanics of building and testing trading strategies. I care about rigor as much as intuition. I want to understand why a model works, not just what it does.

Outside of finance, I follow physics and macroeconomics closely, and I'm drawn to data visualization as a way of making complex ideas legible.

- Currently working on portpy-quant, a Python package for portfolio analytics
- Working through a self-directed program in quantitative finance, from first principles to applied strategy
- Experimenting with systematic and algorithmic trading, including live paper-trading workflows
- Always learning, currently deepening my knowledge of stochastic calculus and market microstructure
- Happy to talk about anything at the intersection of finance, math, and code

### Featured Project

**[portpy-quant](https://github.com/Arthur-Faugeron/PortPy)**
A Python package for portfolio analytics, built to make portfolio construction, risk analysis, and performance attribution more accessible and transparent.

**[One Company A Day](https://arthur-faugeron.github.io/OCAD/)**
A library of daily reports of companies listed in Chile and around the world for people to learn about the company, know how it is doing, and hopefully giving some insights about investment opportunities.

**[Literature Review](https://arthur-faugeron.github.io/literature-review/)**
A library of daily macro-financial papers reviews to help people understand, to enrich with knowledge, and to give friendly access to macro-financial literature.
